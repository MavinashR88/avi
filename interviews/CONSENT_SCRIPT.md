# Consent Script — Discovery Interviews

Three places where consent / framing appears. Use this language verbatim unless a specific wording feels off.

## 1. Zoom "Recording disclaimer" (in-app modal, shown to every participant when recording starts)

> This call is being recorded for our internal product research notes. The recording and transcript are kept private to our small team, names and handles are redacted before any quotes are shared internally, and the raw files are deleted within 14 days of the interview. Click "Continue" to consent and join the recorded session, or "Leave Meeting" to decline.

## 2. Calendly booking-form note (right above the "Schedule Event" button)

> **Heads-up on recording:** we record these calls so we can take faithful notes and re-listen for nuance — the recording is private to our team, deleted within 14 days, and any quotes we share internally are anonymized. The booking form has a yes/no consent question; we won't proceed without an affirmative answer.

## 3. Verbal opening (CEO reads at the start of the call — ~30 seconds)

> Hey — really appreciate you making time. Quick housekeeping before we dive in:
>
> 1. I'm going to hit record in a second. The recording stays with our two-person team, gets transcribed for notes, and we delete the raw file within 14 days. Anything we quote internally gets anonymized.
> 2. There are no wrong answers. I'm genuinely trying to learn how proposal-writing actually plays out for you day to day — the boring parts especially.
> 3. If anything feels too personal or under-NDA, just say "skip" and I'll move on.
>
> Sound good? Cool — hitting record.

## 4. Verbal close (CEO reads at the end of the call — ~15 seconds)

> Last thing — I'll send you a quick summary of what I heard within a week, just to make sure I didn't misquote you. If we end up building something here, I'd love to come back to you as one of the first 5 design partners — totally no obligation.

---

## If consent is declined

If a participant declines recording either at booking time or on the call:

- Don't record. Stop the Zoom recording immediately if it was started.
- Offer to take notes by hand and continue the conversation if they're still willing.
- If they decline the call entirely, refund any incentive promised and thank them.
- Log the decline in `interviews/notes/2026-05-XX-declines.md` (no PII) so we can track decline rate as a funnel metric.
